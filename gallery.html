Orthodontic Treatment

Straightening teeth isn't just about aesthetics — it improves bite function, oral hygiene, and long-term dental health. At DEA Dent, we use modern fixed and removable systems tailored to each patient's anatomy and goals.

✨

Prosthetic Dentistry

From single crowns to full arch restorations, our prosthetic solutions are crafted for natural appearance and lasting durability. We work with certified dental laboratories to ensure every case meets the highest standards.

🔩

Implantology

Dental implants offer a permanent, bone-anchored solution for missing teeth. Our implantologists perform thorough planning using 3D imaging to ensure precision placement and optimal osseointegration outcomes.

🔬

Endodontics

Root canal treatment performed with care and precision. Using rotary systems and digital apex locators, our endodontists preserve natural teeth that would otherwise require extraction — maximizing your long-term dental health.
